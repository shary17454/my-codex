Batal Al-Droob 2.7

New in this build: ten interface languages, explained search results, and a drivetrain category.

Please test the languages:
- Open the language menu and switch between Arabic, English, Spanish, French, German, Russian, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, Turkish, and Hindi.
- Confirm the layout is right-to-left only for Arabic and left-to-right for every other language.
- Confirm any untranslated string falls back to English (never to Arabic or a raw key).
- Note: catalog part names stay Arabic/English because the source catalogs are published that way.

Please test search and fitment:
- Search a full part number and confirm the top result shows a "Number match" badge.
- Search by description (for example "brake pads") and confirm a "Description match" or "Synonym match" badge appears.
- Enter your vehicle details, then search: matching parts should rank higher and show "Fits your vehicle".
- Open the categories bar and confirm the "Drivetrain" category appears with its parts (these previously fell under "General").

Please test part requests:
- Try saving a request without a part number/name, or without a generation/year: it should be blocked with a checklist of what is missing.
- Once complete, the request preview appears live and ends with a line asking the supplier to confirm availability and price.

Please test the original catalog:
- Open the catalog library and confirm on-demand PDF downloads with visible progress.
- Confirm a search result jumps to the targeted PDF page and that sharing works.
- Test Dynamic Type, Dark Mode, and offline/error states.

Note: Downloading the original PDFs requires iOS 26 or later, while indexed search remains available on older systems (minimum iOS 17).
