Batal Al-Droob 2.8

This build carries everything in 2.7 (ten interface languages, explained search
results, the drivetrain category) and adds a noticeable speed-up in search and on the
home screen, plus real translations for the eight non-Arabic languages instead of an
English fallback.

Please test responsiveness (the main point of this build):
- Open the Catalog and type in the search field letter by letter: typing should not
  stutter and results should not lag behind.
- Scroll the results list quickly: it should stay smooth while the reason badges show.
- Open the Home screen: the category counts and all four generation cards should
  appear immediately, with no hitch.
- Open the catalog library and open a large file: an "Opening the catalog..."
  indicator appears and the Done button stays tappable while it loads. Previously the
  sheet froze until the whole file was parsed.

Please test the languages:
- Open the language menu and switch between Arabic, English, Spanish, French, German,
  Russian, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, Turkish, and Hindi.
- Most important: pick a language other than Arabic or English, then delete and
  reinstall the app to see the first-run welcome screen. It should appear in your
  chosen language; in the previous build it stayed English.
- Confirm the category names (Engine, Cooling, Brake, Drivetrain, ...) and the search
  reason badges (Number match, Partial number, Synonym match) appear in your language
  rather than English.
- Send the app to the app switcher: the "Content protected" shield should be in your
  language.
- Confirm the layout is right-to-left only for Arabic and left-to-right for every other
  language, and watch the chevron in the catalog library rows: it must mirror with the
  layout direction.
- Intended behaviour: part names, part numbers, and store names stay Arabic/English,
  because the source catalogs are published that way and OEM numbers are
  language-neutral.

Please test maintenance and requests:
- Add a maintenance entry: its date should now appear in the log. The date was always
  recorded but never displayed.
- Save a part request: the request date should appear under the part number.
- Try saving a request with no part number/name, or with no generation/year: it should
  block you and list exactly what is missing.

Please test search and fitment:
- Search a full part number and confirm the top result shows a "Number match" badge.
- Search an Arabic description (for example: فحمات فرامل) and confirm a "Description
  match" or "Synonym match" badge.
- Enter your vehicle details, then search: matching parts should rank higher and show
  a "Fits your vehicle" badge.
- Toggle "Filter results by my vehicle" on and off: the result count should change
  immediately.

Note: downloading the original catalogs from Apple requires iOS 26 or later. Indexed
search and catalog data still work on iOS 17 and later.
